# Head Admin election rules (draft)

## Cycle
- Each term is about 3 months. Nominations open roughly 3 weeks before the term ends and stay open 14 days.
- Voting opens when nominations close and runs 7 days.
- The result is posted in #elections and roles change the moment the vote closes.

## Who can run and vote
- In the server for at least 30 days. Staff applications (`/apply`) have no tenure or link requirement.
- Minecraft account linked through DiscordSRV.
- One vote per person. No voting for yourself.
- At least two candidates must run, or the election is cancelled and the Owner decides.

## Ballot
- Pick a 1st, 2nd and 3rd choice. Points are 3, 2 and 1.
- Ballots are anonymous and cannot be changed. The bot records who voted separately from what was voted.
  The person hosting the bot can read its database, so anonymity depends on that person not looking.

## Ties
1. Most points wins.
2. If equal, most 1st-place votes wins.
3. If still equal, most 2nd-place votes wins.
4. If still equal, the tied candidates go to a 48 hour runoff with the same rules.
5. If the runoff ties, the Owner flips a coin in the voice channel or a public stream, and posts the result.

## Handover
- The winner gets Head Admin. The outgoing Head Admin becomes Admin, or a player if they chose to step down.
- DiscordSRV syncs the Discord role to the in-game group.

## Powers and oversight
- Head Admin answers to the Owner. Sensible decisions stand.
- Every admin action that affects players is posted to #admin-actions with a reason (`/adminlog`).
- The first term is appointed by the Owner. Elections start with the second term.

### Head Admin can do without asking
- Add or remove Admins and Staff, with a reason posted publicly.
- Change gameplay settings that members can see and reverse: spawn, events, build rules, shop prices.
- Ban or mute players for breaking posted rules.
- Run events, wipe small areas, and update the server rules.

### Head Admin needs Owner approval first
- A world reset, map wipe or Minecraft version change.
- Anything that costs money: hosting, paid plugin licences, Discord setup.
- Giving items, ranks or permissions to anyone for free outside an announced event.
- A permanent ban of anyone who is not a clear rule-breaker, or any ban of staff.
- Changing these rules or the election rules.

### Head Admin can never
- Take or spawn items, ranks or permissions for themselves or their friends.
- Use admin powers to win a dispute they are personally part of.
- Delete or edit the #admin-actions log.
- Ignore a vote result or hold power past the term.

### Sensible versus crazy
An idea is sensible if it passes all four checks:
1. It applies to everyone, not one person or group.
2. It can be undone.
3. It costs no money and no one's progress.
4. The reason fits in two sentences and is posted publicly.

An idea that fails any check is crazy. Examples: wiping one player's base, giving a friend free diamonds, deleting the log.

### Disputes and removal
- Disputes go to the Owner, who decides within 48 hours and posts the answer.
- The Owner can reverse any action and posts the reason in #admin-actions.
- Repeated or deliberate breaches get the Head Admin removed mid-term, with a public reason. The runner-up from the last vote takes over, or the Owner appoints a replacement if the runner-up declines.
