# Refract SMP is coming back

It's the successor to Prism SMP, and we're rebuilding it properly this time.

## Where things stand
The server is being built now. It won't be open to the public until **early December**. Until then I'm working on it, and I'll post progress here.

I'm taking the Head Admin role for the first 3 months to steer the creation of the new server. After that, you choose.

## How leadership works
Prism ran into trouble with too many owners and too little to do. Refract has one owner (me), and a Head Admin who changes every 3 months.

- Near the end of each 3 month term, anyone eligible can put their name forward for Head Admin.
- Two weeks later, voting opens. You pick a 1st, 2nd and 3rd choice. Votes are anonymous.
- The winner becomes Head Admin. The previous one goes back to Admin, or to a regular player if they want out.
- The Head Admin answers to me, but sensible ideas and changes go ahead. Admin actions are posted publicly in #admin-actions with a reason.

The first vote is in **late January**.

To run for Head Admin or vote, you need:
- 30 days in this server
- your Minecraft account linked

## Joining the server
We intend to open Refract to the public with **no application needed** to play. In return, moderation will be strict on new players and on anyone who hasn't linked their Minecraft account to Discord. Link your account early.

## We need staff
A server this size needs more than me. If you want to help moderate, or you know someone good, join the server and use `/apply`. It's a short form. An admin reviews it and replies. You can apply the day you join.

## Tell us what you want
We're deciding on plugins and the direction of the server now, while it's still being built. If there's something you want, like a plugin, a game mode or a style of play, say so in #feedback before the server opens.
